# Phase 6.16 AI Governance & Control Layer

## Objective

Define the governance layer that controls how Bodega6A AI operates, makes decisions, and performs actions.

## Governance Flow

AI Request

↓

Policy Evaluation

↓

Permission Check

↓

Risk Assessment

↓

Decision Approval

↓

Controlled Execution

↓

Audit Record

## Governance Principles

- Every important action must be traceable.
- Autonomous behavior must have defined limits.
- Sensitive operations require approval.
- Security rules override optimization goals.

## Status

Governance framework prepared.
