# Phase 6.14 AI Knowledge Evolution

## Objective

Prepare the knowledge evolution layer that allows Bodega6A AI to maintain better project understanding over time.

## Knowledge lifecycle

Project Data

↓

Extraction

↓

Organization

↓

Knowledge Storage

↓

Retrieval

↓

Reasoning Support

↓

Continuous Improvement

## Capabilities

The AI should be able to:

- Maintain technical context.
- Remember previous decisions.
- Avoid repeating known mistakes.
- Improve future recommendations.
- Build project-specific knowledge.

## Safety

Knowledge updates must be traceable and reviewable.
