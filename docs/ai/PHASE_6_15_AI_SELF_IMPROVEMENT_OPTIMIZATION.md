# Phase 6.15 AI Self-Improvement & Optimization

## Objective

Prepare the optimization layer that allows Bodega6A AI to evaluate its own assistance quality and improve future behavior.

## Optimization Cycle

AI Action

↓

Result Analysis

↓

Performance Evaluation

↓

Improvement Detection

↓

Optimization Proposal

↓

Validation

↓

Future Application

## Capabilities

The AI should be able to:

- Detect repeated inefficiencies.
- Identify weak recommendations.
- Improve planning quality.
- Refine technical suggestions.
- Learn from validated outcomes.

## Safety

Self-improvement must be controlled.

The AI can propose improvements, but critical behavior changes require validation.
