# AI Autonomy Levels

## Level 0 - Assistance

AI provides information and recommendations.

## Level 1 - Planning

AI creates technical plans and impact analysis.

## Level 2 - Controlled Execution

AI prepares changes through approved workflows.

## Level 3 - Supervised Automation

AI can execute predefined safe actions with monitoring.

## Level 4 - Advanced Autonomy

Requires stronger governance, auditing, and approval systems.

## Current Target

Controlled execution with human approval.
